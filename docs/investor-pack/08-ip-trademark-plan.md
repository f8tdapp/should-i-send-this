# BetweenLines AI — IP and Trade Mark Plan

**Private preparation document. Not legal advice; review with qualified IP counsel.**

## Protection thesis

The broad idea of using AI to check message tone may be difficult to own or exclude others from. The protectable value is more specific: the brand, named framework, code, original copy and visual assets, prompt expression, private scoring/classification system, evaluation materials, product know-how, and trade secret materials. Protection also depends on clean ownership and disciplined confidentiality.

## IP assets inventory

- Layered-line/dot visual mark, icons, color system, and generated social artwork.
- Taglines and distinctive product language.

### Trade secret materials

- Calibration results and unpublished evaluation sets.

- Strategic integration, pricing, acquisition, and partner materials.

## Trade mark plan

### BetweenLines AI

1. Record exact proposed goods/services, territories, owners, and launch plans.
2. Conduct web, company-name, domain, app-store, social, and formal register searches.
3. Ask counsel about distinctiveness, conflicting marks, classes, word versus logo filings, and the effect of “AI.”
4. File in priority territories only after clearance and commercial review.
5. Use ™ while an unregistered claim is appropriate; use ® only after registration in the relevant jurisdiction.

### Perception Gap™

1. Search existing uses across communication, psychology, marketing, research, and software.
2. Ask whether the phrase is sufficiently distinctive for the intended goods/services or may be descriptive.
3. Document first use, specimens, consistent capitalization, and connection to BetweenLines AI.
4. Consider brand architecture and filing order with counsel before wider publication.

## Ownership and confidentiality

- Confirm the repository, domains, provider accounts, analytics, brand files, prompts, and documentation are owned by the intended founder/company entity.
- Keep an authorship and contribution record for code, copy, design, and AI-assisted assets.
- Require contractor/employee agreements with confidentiality, present-tense IP assignment, moral-rights treatment where applicable, and confirmation of third-party/open-source inputs.
- Obtain assignments for any earlier contributions that are not already clearly owned.
- Use NDAs for genuinely confidential investor, advisor, contractor, partner, and acquisition discussions when appropriate; do not treat an NDA as a substitute for access control.
- Limit prompt, scoring, analytics, customer, and provider access to people who need it.

## Attorney questions

- Is BetweenLines AI clear and registrable in the priority territories/classes?
- Is Perception Gap registrable, and how should ™ be used before filing/registration?
- Who currently owns every code, design, copy, prompt, and domain asset?
- Are generated icons/artwork safe to use and protect under applicable terms and law?
- Which open-source and provider licences require attribution or impose constraints?
- What contractor/employee assignments are missing?
- What should remain trade secret versus published marketing explanation?
- Are product claims, privacy statements, disclaimers, and acquisition materials appropriately qualified?
- What recordkeeping is needed for first use, authorship, and diligence?
- Which filings are worth their cost at the present validation stage?
