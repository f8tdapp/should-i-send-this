## Possible future scenarios

### 1. No raise / bootstrapped

Use founder time and controlled operating spend to confirm production readiness, run a small soft launch, measure repeat use and quality, and reach the first monetization decision. Benefits include control and simplicity; constraints include slower legal, security, design, and distribution work.

### 2. Small angel/advisor round

Amount and instrument: **TBD**. Appropriate only if one or more contributors bring unusually relevant operating knowledge, distribution access, legal/IP help, or product mentorship. Keep ownership, governance, information rights, and administrative burden proportionate to the evidence stage.

### 3. £25k–£50k validation round

Possible purpose: fund a defined validation window covering legal/IP groundwork, privacy/security review, product polish, measured acquisition experiments, infrastructure, and limited developer support. Success milestones and runway assumptions: **TBD before any outreach**.

### 4. £100k–£150k pre-seed-style round

Possible purpose: accelerate a validated product with evidence of repeat usage, trusted outputs, credible unit economics, and a specific integration or growth thesis. This scenario should not be pursued merely to fund broad feature development. Valuation, instrument, dilution, milestones, and hiring plan: **TBD with professional advice**.

## Preferred investor/advisor criteria

- Messaging, email, communication, or productivity product experience.
- Applied AI/product judgment, especially evaluation and model economics.
- Consumer app growth and retention experience without manipulative engagement tactics.
- Privacy, trust, safety, security, or regulated-data experience.
- Acquisition, integration, partnership, or operator background in relevant platforms.
- Willingness to respect a focused product, evidence-led roadmap, and founder ownership.

## Questions before any raise

- What evidence cannot be obtained reasonably through bootstrapping?
- What exact milestone does the money buy, by what date?
- Is the strongest path standalone subscription, integration, licensing, partnership, or acquisition?
- Are retention, quality, cost, and privacy metrics reliable enough for diligence?
- Is IP ownership clean and are core names clear enough to invest behind?
- What control, dilution, reporting, and time cost accompanies each option?
- Does a prospective investor materially improve the probability of the chosen path?
